# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mission Control operators and administrators managing agents, scheduled work,
boards, and their own mobile applications. They need to inspect current state,
understand failures, and recover without losing their place.

## Product Purpose

An operational dashboard for OpenClaw. Mobile Applications brings together
App Store and Google Play reviews, ratings, official reports, and sentiment
digests. Cached data remains useful while external sources refresh.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, consistent. Preserve the existing dashboard style and theme
choices; improvements should make operational information easier to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid a broad visual redesign, decorative motion, or replacing useful cached
content with a full-screen spinner during routine refreshes. Do not present
unknown or failed work as successful.

## Design Principles

- Keep values, source labels, and freshness consistent.
- Preserve the user's current app, filters, and reading position.
- Explain failures where they occur and provide a recovery action.
- Distinguish initial loading, live refresh, and background report work.
- Reuse the established dashboard controls and theme tokens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, readable contrast, and reduced motion. Announce
loading and error states without relying on color alone. Keep controls usable
with long app names, international reviews, and narrow screens.
